One run, step by step

  1. The Growth Lead pastes company websites and clicks Run.
  2. Signal reads each company's homepage, and a few more pages if it needs them.
  3. Gemini pulls out the key facts. Each fact needs a quote from the site.
  4. Hunter adds the company size and finds a named person to email.
  5. Code decides Send, Hold or Drop, and shows the rule behind it.
  6. For a Send, Gemini writes an email to that person by first name.
  7. Code checks every quote is on the site. If not, the email is blocked.
  8. The email goes to the Growth Lead's own inbox.
  9. The Growth Lead reviews it, and rejected companies are skipped next time.

What we changed

  • Emails now start with "Hi <first name>,".
  • Send emails go out automatically, no click needed.
  • Companies that already have SOC 2 are caught, not pitched as new.
  • Hunter fills in company size when the site doesn't say.
  • Email is sent through Resend.
